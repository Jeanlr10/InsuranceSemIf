SYNTHETIC. This is a fictional sample policy created for a software demo, not a real product, and it contains no data about any real person.

Plan name: Harborlight Choice Sample Plan

§1.1 Medically Necessary
Covered services must be medically necessary to diagnose or treat an illness, injury, or impairment. Care that is not medically necessary is not paid under this sample policy. Medical necessity is decided using accepted clinical standards.

§1.2 Eligibility
A member must be enrolled through the demo enrollment screen and reside in the fictional service area shown in the sample records. Coverage begins on the sample effective date listed in the demo record.

§2.1 Preventive Care
Wellness exams are covered once per calendar year with no member cost. Immunizations such as flu shots are covered. Screenings such as a screening colonoscopy are covered at the recommended interval.

§2.2 Inpatient Hospital Care
Covered inpatient care includes a semi-private room, meals, nursing care, and drugs given in the hospital. The member pays the daily coinsurance shown in the sample benefit table.

§2.3 Outpatient Surgery
Surgery performed in an outpatient surgical center is covered at the outpatient benefit level. Surgery done mainly to change appearance falls under §4.1 and is not paid under this clause.

§2.4 Emergency Services
Emergency care is covered when a prudent layperson would reasonably believe the symptoms call for immediate attention. Emergency room care and emergency ambulance transport are covered at the in-network level no matter which provider treats the member.

§2.5 Prescription Drugs
Covered drugs are listed in the plan formulary. Specialty drugs need prior authorization before they are filled. A drug that is not on the formulary may be covered after an exception request is approved.

§2.6 Mental Health and Substance Use
Outpatient therapy, inpatient psychiatric care, and substance use treatment are covered on the same terms as other covered conditions. The member pays the standard cost sharing for these services.

§2.7 Maternity and Newborn Care
Prenatal visits, delivery, and postpartum care are covered. Routine care of the newborn is covered for the first thirty-one days after birth.

§2.8 Rehabilitation Therapy
Physical therapy, occupational therapy, and speech therapy are covered when ordered by a provider. Coverage is limited to 30 visits per calendar year combined across all three therapies.

§2.9 Imaging and Laboratory
X-rays and lab tests are covered at the outpatient benefit level. MRI, CT, and PET scans need prior authorization under §3.1.

§2.10 Durable Medical Equipment
Items such as crutches, wheelchairs, and home oxygen equipment are covered when prescribed. Any item over $1,000 needs prior authorization.

§3.1 Prior Authorization
A provider requests prior authorization by submitting the sample request form with clinical notes before the service is scheduled. If a service that requires prior authorization is performed without it, the claim is denied unless the delay was beyond the provider's control. Urgent requests are reviewed within twenty-four hours and standard requests within five business days.

§3.2 Network and Out-of-Network Providers
Network providers are paid at the full benefit level and the member pays the lower network cost sharing. Out-of-network providers are paid at a lower level and the member may owe the difference. Emergency care is the exception and is always paid at the in-network level under §2.4.

§3.3 Deductible and Out-of-Pocket Maximum
The member pays the sample deductible each calendar year before the plan pays most covered services. Once the member reaches the out-of-pocket maximum, the plan pays covered services in full for the rest of the year.

§4.1 Cosmetic and Reconstructive Procedures
Procedures done mainly to change appearance, such as rhinoplasty or facial implants, are not paid. Reconstructive surgery after a mastectomy is paid, including surgery on the other breast to create symmetry.

§4.2 Experimental and Investigational Treatment
Treatment that is experimental or investigational is not paid. A drug used outside its approved purpose is paid only when the off-label use is supported by recognized clinical evidence and appears in the sample coverage criteria.

§4.3 Dental and Vision
Routine dental care and routine eye exams for glasses are not paid under this sample policy. Dental care for an accidental injury and pediatric vision screening are paid as shown in the sample benefit table.

§4.4 Weight Loss and Bariatric Surgery
Bariatric surgery is considered for members with a body mass index of 40 or higher, or 35 or higher with a related condition such as diabetes. The member must complete a six-month supervised weight loss program before surgery is reviewed. Prior authorization is required in every case.

§4.5 Fertility and Infertility Treatment
In vitro fertilization and egg retrieval are not paid. Testing to diagnose infertility is paid at the outpatient benefit level.

§5.1 Claim Filing
A claim must be filed within 180 days from the date of service. A claim filed later is not paid unless the delay was beyond the member's control.

§5.2 Appeals
A member may ask for a review of a denied claim within 180 days of the denial notice. The plan completes the first review within thirty days and sends a written sample decision. A second independent review is available after the first review is finished.

§5.3 Coordination of Benefits
If a member has coverage under more than one sample policy, this plan pays after the other plan when it is the secondary payer. Payments are combined so the member does not receive more than the covered cost.
